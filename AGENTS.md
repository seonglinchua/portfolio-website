# Nezha Systems Design Notes

- Treat the selected light, editorial flat-illustration direction as the visual source of truth.
- Use Newsreader for display typography and DM Sans for interface/body text.
- Keep imagery flat, two-dimensional, and editorial. Avoid photorealism, 3D rendering, generic stock imagery, and cyberpunk effects.
- Core palette: warm white, deep navy, electric blue, cyan, teal, with coral used only as a small signal accent.
- Prefer whitespace, typography, alignment, and thin rules over cards, borders, and shadows.
- New content belongs in `src/data`; reusable interface belongs in `src/components`; full homepage regions belong in `src/sections`.
- Preserve accessibility, reduced-motion behavior, keyboard focus, dark theme support, and mobile navigation.
